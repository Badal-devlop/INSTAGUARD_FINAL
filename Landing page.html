// How it works

Four steps.
Zero friction.

InstaguardAI runs silently in the background. You scroll Instagram normally — it does the work.

01
👁️

Watch & Extract

MutationObserver detects new posts as you scroll. Extracts captions, alt text, image URLs, and video thumbnails automatically.

02
🧠

AI Analysis

Gemini AI analyses the extracted content for misinformation patterns, misleading language, deepfake indicators, and fabricated claims.

03
🔍

Fact Cross-Reference

Cross-references against NewsAPI and PIB India's official fact-check RSS feed for real-world verification.

04
🏷️

Badge & Panel

A subtle inline badge appears on flagged content. One click opens the full fact-check panel with confidence score, reasoning, and sources.

Badge Types

🔴 Likely Fake
🟡 Verify This
🔵 Satire
🟢 Appears Credible

// Installation

Up in 60 seconds.

No store. No payment. Just load it directly in Chrome.

1
Download the extension Clone or download ZIP from github.com/Badal-devlop/INSTAGUARD_FINAL
2
Open Chrome Extensions Go to chrome://extensions in your browser
3
Enable Developer Mode Toggle Developer mode in the top-right corner
4
Load Unpacked Click Load unpacked → select the instaguardai folder
5
Open Instagram & Scroll Visit instagram.com — InstaguardAI activates automatically
